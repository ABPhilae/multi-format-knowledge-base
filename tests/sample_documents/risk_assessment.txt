H1 2025 Risk Assessment — APAC Region

Executive Summary

This document presents the H1 2025 risk assessment for BNP Paribas Internal Audit APAC operations. The overall APAC risk rating is Elevated, up from Moderate in the previous assessment, primarily driven by increased regulatory scrutiny and trade reconciliation gaps.

Critical Findings

Finding 1: Insufficient trade reconciliation controls in Hong Kong
- Severity: Critical
- Owner: Li Wei
- Deadline: March 31, 2026
- Description: The Trade Reconciliation System (TRS) has not been updated since 2023 and does not support the latest cross-border transaction formats required by HKMA.

Finding 2: Outdated AML screening parameters
- Severity: Critical
- Owner: Li Wei
- Deadline: March 31, 2026
- Description: The AML Screening Engine v4.1 uses parameters last calibrated in Q2 2024. Updated screening rules from FATF are not yet implemented.

Finding 3: Technology risk assessment framework gaps
- Severity: Significant
- Owner: Regional Technology Risk Team
- Deadline: June 30, 2026
- Description: The current technology risk framework does not adequately cover cloud services and third-party API dependencies.

Regional Summary

Hong Kong: 3 critical findings, 2 significant findings. Primary risks in trade reconciliation and AML controls.
Singapore: 2 significant findings. Primary risks in technology controls and data governance.
